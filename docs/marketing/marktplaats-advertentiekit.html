What we found

How the category looks today

Most ads in the Rotterdam results come from single-person shops. They sound alike: "snel en vakkundig", "scherpe prijzen", "gratis prijsopgave". Almost none show proof of quality. The national chains sell something different: certainty. They promise guarantees, direct handling with the insurer, replacement transport and measurable quality. Colour King can combine both: local and personal, with the proof of a chain.

Titles are keyword lists

Examples: "schadeherstel, Auto spuiten, Lakschade, Blikschade, Taxatie". Ads like this are easy to find but look cheap, and none of them names a benefit for the customer.

A price hook works

The top ad in the category opens with "Auto spuiten vanaf €250 gratis prijsopgave". A "from" price earns clicks, but only use one if you can honour it.

Chains sell certainty

ABS promotes a mobility guarantee, a BOVAG/brand guarantee, direct handling with insurers and lease companies, and a customer rating. Schadegarant promotes a lower excess, free replacement transport and pick-up and delivery.

Gap: proof

No local ad mentions a colour-match measurement, a straightening-bench report, a paint-thickness measurement or a written guarantee. Colour King already has all of these on its website. The ads below build on them.

Gap: the first 160 characters

Marktplaats shows about 160 characters of the description in search results. Most competitors spend them on filler. Every ad below puts the promise, the proof and the location in that first stretch.

Gap: one ad per service

Customers search for "spot repair", "uitdeuken" or "hagelschade", not for a company name. Separate ads per service, all linking back to Colour King, win more searches than one general ad.

Check before posting

These ads only use claims from Colour King's own website. A few of those claims contradict each other or may be placeholders. Misleading advertising is fined by ACM and hurts your reviews, so confirm these first:

  • BOVAG / RDW / Erkend Leerbedrijf: the trust section of the website lists them, but they may be placeholders. They are left out of the ads. Add them only if they are real.
  • 2-year guarantee: the website promises this on paint and structural work. The ads rely on it.
  • Opening hours: the footer says Sat 09:00–13:00, the FAQ says "Saturday by appointment", and another page says closing time is 17:00. The ads use Ma–Vr 08:00–17:30, za op afspraak.
  • Volume: the website says both "4,800 vehicles" and "5,000th repair (2024)". The ads say ruim 4.800, the safest of the two.
  • Replacement transport: the ads say "beschikbaar" (available), not "gratis" (free), unless it is always free.
  • Spot repair turnaround: the website says "binnen enkele uren" (within a few hours). The ad says "vaak dezelfde dag klaar" (often ready the same day).
